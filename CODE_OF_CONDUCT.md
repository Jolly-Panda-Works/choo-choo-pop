# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We want everyone to feel welcome here, whatever their age, background, identity or level of experience. Be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and sexual or violent content aren't allowed. This is a game for children, so keep the project's spaces suitable for them.

## Reporting

To report a problem, write to contact@useffarahmand.com. Reports are kept private, and the maintainer may remove content or block people who break these rules.
