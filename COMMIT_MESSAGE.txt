feat(stage): panda-house levels on a railway for forest stages

Forest (green) stages now show their 20 levels as panda houses connected by a
railway, level 1 at the bottom up to level 20 (src/ui/ForestTrack.jsx).

- Houses by difficulty: easy = leaf roof, normal = bamboo, hard = flowers,
  super hard (level 20) = station with clock.
- Locked levels are drawn in grey with a padlock.
- The blank plaque of every house holds the 3 stars: earned ones gold, the
  rest grey (all grey while locked).
- The screen opens scrolled to the level the player is on; the panda marker and
  shine stay on the current level.
- Other themes keep the old bubble grid (Stage.jsx falls back to it).
- Add node art (art/map/node/*.webp, resized from the delivered PNGs).
