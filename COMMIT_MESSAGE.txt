feat: kids edition (age 7) with 3x3 tutorial, 20 levels, EN/FA builds and new branding

Gameplay
- Replace the 10 old levels with a 20-level Part 1. Levels 1-5 are a guided
  3x3 tutorial (drag, turns, numbers, erase a wrong rail, star tile) with an
  animated finger, pulsing clue/star rings and short coach texts. Level 4 starts
  with a deliberately wrong rail so the child learns to slide back.
- Make difficulty honest: levels 6-20 are accepted only when the solver's search
  node count falls inside a per-slot band (new solveStats()), so the curve only
  goes up; boards grow from 3x4 to a max of 6x6 with helper star tiles.
  Labels are now tutorial / easy / medium / hard.
- Hints are free and no-penalty in the tutorial; +1 hint per 2 finished levels.
- Camera now centres on the real content box (board + gates + clues).

Editions
- Build-time edition (VITE_EDITION): "en" = Choo Choo Pop, "fa" = هو هو چی چی
  (bundled Vazirmatn, Persian digits, RTL). Language switch removed.
