fix(android): build failed with "Value is null" on versionCode in app/build.gradle

Groovy read `versionCode (x).toInteger()` as `versionCode(x).toInteger()`: the
String went to the versionCode method and toInteger() ran on its null result.
Use `versionCode = ...` and `versionName = ...`, so both are set as properties.
Same failure for the en and fa jobs.

fix(stage): soft top and bottom edge on the levels scroll area

The cloud banks only cover part of the edge of the scroll area, so houses and rails
were still cut off by a hard line right under the stage title. The scroll area now has
a gradient mask (transparent -> opaque over ~14% of its height, top and bottom), so the
content fades out before the clipping edge.

fix(popups): shop and coins popups open on the current screen and close back to it

Tapping the coin chip (top right) used to switch to a separate "shop" / "coins"
screen; closing it went through the screen history and the previous screen was
mounted again (a running game or an open start popup was lost). The shop and coin
popups are now overlays in App.jsx: they open on top of the screen the player is on
(home, map, levels, game) and the X (or the Android back button) just closes them,
leaving that screen untouched. Shop -> coins switches between the two popups.

feat(stage): houses fly in/out, camera glide, drag instead of scrollbar, cloud edges

- Changing stage (pager arrows): the houses of the old stage fly out, the new
  stage opens and its houses fly in (bottom house first, alternating sides).
  Respects prefers-reduced-motion.
- Camera: the view starts on the first house and glides to the house the player
  has to play (current level of this stage), or stays on the first house when the
  stage has no current level. Grabbing the view (mouse, wheel, touch) stops it.
- No scrollbar on the levels screen; the view is dragged with the mouse / pen
  (grab cursor, inertia, the click that ends a drag is swallowed). Touch keeps
  native scrolling. Shared hook useDragScroll in ui/MapKit.jsx.
- Soft edges: cloud banks (art/map/ui/clouds-band.webp) cover the top and bottom
  of the scroll area, so houses and rails fade into the sky.

fix(ci): stop rewriting android/app/build.gradle (Android build failed)

The "Configure Android edition" step ran scripts/android-edition.mjs, which
replaced the head of the committed android/app/build.gradle with its own
(variable renamed to `editionName`, kotlin plugin and src/edition-* source sets
dropped) but kept the tail, which still reads `edition`. Gradle then failed with:

  Could not get unknown property 'edition' for object of type
  DefaultDependencyHandler   (android/app/build.gradle line 50)

build.gradle is already edition-aware and null-safe (-PEDITION, -PVERSION_CODE,
-PVERSION_NAME all have defaults), so the script is obsolete: remove the
workflow step and the script. Same failure for both the en and fa jobs.
